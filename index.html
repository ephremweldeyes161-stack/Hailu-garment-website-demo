Who We Are

About Hailu Garment

Hailu Garment is a leading Ethiopian garment manufacturing company with over 10 years of experience in the apparel industry. We specialize in designing, manufacturing, and supplying high-quality uniforms, workwear, school uniforms, hospital apparel, security uniforms, and customized textile products.

Through a strong commitment to quality, professionalism, and timely delivery, Hailu Garment has built a solid reputation as a reliable manufacturing partner for many of Ethiopia's most respected institutions.

We provide complete garment manufacturing solutions, covering the entire production process — from consultation and design to manufacturing, quality control, and delivery.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver high-quality, durable, and affordable garments that meet the unique needs of our customers while maintaining excellence in craftsmanship, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of Ethiopia's most trusted and innovative garment manufacturers, recognized for quality, reliability, and sustainable growth.
